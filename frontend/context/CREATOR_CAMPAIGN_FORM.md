Designing the campaign creation page for **Kern** requires balancing "Founder-level" financial control with "Creative-level" clarity. Since we are using a **Serif aesthetic**, the form should not feel like a chaotic survey; it should feel like you are drafting a **Formal Deed**.

To make this "Trustless," we must shift from open-ended text to **Machine-Readable Requirements**. If a creator writes "make it funny," the AI can't verify that. If they select "Face Visible," the AI can.

### 1. The UX Flow: A Multi-Step "Drafting" Process

Instead of one long, intimidating form, use a **3-step wizard**. This makes the "Vault Deposit" (the scary part) the final, satisfying click.

* **Step 1: The Thesis (Basic Info)**
* **Step 2: The Mandate (Requirements & AI Rules)**
* **Step 3: The Treasury (Budget & SOL Deposit)**

---

### 2. Form Field Breakdown

#### Step 1: The Thesis

* **Campaign Title:** (e.g., *"The Podcast Launch Program"*)
* **Social Targets:** (Multi-select) TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels.
* **Source Material:** A URL field for the "Source VOD" (YouTube link) or a file upload for the raw footage. *Kern's AI will index this specific footage.*

#### Step 2: The Mandate (The "AI Review" Setup)

This is where the creator defines the "Proof of Work." To stay trustless, you should provide **Requirement Tags**:

* **Standardized AI Checks (Checkboxes):**
* [ ] **Face Recognition:** Only pay if the creator’s face is detected.
* [ ] **Audio Match:** Only pay if the audio is from the source file (prevents generic stolen content).
* [ ] **Subtitles/Captions:** AI checks for text overlays (high-quality shorts usually have them).
* [ ] **Minimum Duration:** (e.g., 15 seconds).


* **Custom Requirements (The "Human" Clause):**
* A text area for "Creator Guidelines" (e.g., *"Don't use swearing," "Must use my brand colors"*).
* *UX Note:* Add a disclaimer: *"Custom requirements may require manual review/dispute resolution. Standardized checks are paid instantly by AI."*



#### Step 3: The Treasury (The Math)

* **Reward Rate:** $X$ amount of USDC/SOL per 1,000 views.
* **Campaign Budget:** Total amount to lock in the Vault (e.g., 20 SOL).
* **The "Cliff" (Expiry):** When the campaign ends and the creator can withdraw unused funds.

---

### 3. The "Requirement" Strategy: Freedom vs. Automation

To answer your question: **Yes, they should create requirements freely, but you must categorize them.**

**The "Kern" Way:**
Divide the requirements UI into **"Hard Rules"** and **"Soft Style."**

* **Hard Rules (AI-Verified):** These are the checkboxes mentioned above. If a clipper fails these, the system rejects them immediately. No human needed.
* **Soft Style (Human-Verified):** These are the creative notes. The AI will summarize these for the clipper, but it won't "auto-reject" based on them. If a clipper ignores these, the Creator has to use the **Dispute Mechanic** we discussed.

---

### 4. High-End Serif UI Components

Since we are going for the **Kern** look, here are the UI elements you should use for this page:

1. **The "Live Math":**
* As the creator types in the "Budget" and "Rate," a small card calculates the **"Estimated Reach."**
* *Example:* "With 20 SOL and a $1/1k rate, you are funding approximately **5,000,000 views** across your community."

3. **The Typography:**
* Use **Large Serif Numbers** for the budget input.
* Use **Thin Serif Labels** for the form fields.



### 5. Final UX Touch: The "Trust Signal" (LATER)

Before they hit "Deposit & Launch," show them a **"Clipper Visibility"** preview.

> *"Based on your rules, 452 verified clippers in our network are eligible for this campaign. Average payout time: 48.2 hours."*

This gives the creator confidence that if they lock their money, people will actually show up to work.
